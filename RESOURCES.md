# Resources

Every book, video, paper and doc used in this plan, grouped by type. ★ = free online. Lessons link to the specific chapters/videos to use.

## Top picks if you only get a few

- [Brunton & Kutz — *Data-Driven Science and Engineering* (2nd ed., Cambridge)](https://databookuw.com/) — The key book for ML + fluids: SVD/POD, DMD, ROMs, physics-informed ML. Site has videos and code for every section.
- [Thuerey et al. — *Physics-based Deep Learning* (free online)](https://physicsbaseddeeplearning.org/) — Surrogates, PINNs, differentiable physics — with notebooks.
- Versteeg & Malalasekera — *An Introduction to Computational Fluid Dynamics: The Finite Volume Method* (2nd ed.) — The classic FVM book: diffusion, convection schemes, SIMPLE, turbulence.
- [Prince — *Understanding Deep Learning* (free online)](https://udlbook.github.io/udlbook/) — Clear, well-illustrated, with notebooks.
- [Fluid Mechanics 101 (Aidan Wimshurst) — YouTube CFD lectures](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Fluid+Mechanics+101+Aidan+Wimshurst) — Excellent short lectures on FVM, SIMPLE, turbulence models, y+ and wall functions.
- [József Nagy — OpenFOAM tutorials (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCjdgpuxuAxH9BqheyE82Vvw) — The largest set of OpenFOAM video tutorials: setup, meshing, ParaView.
- [Steve Brunton (Eigensteve) — YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@Eigensteve) — Series on fluid dynamics, SVD/POD, DMD, ML for fluids and physics-informed ML.

## Books

- Kundu, Cohen & Dowling — *Fluid Mechanics* (6th ed., Academic Press) — The standard graduate fluid mechanics text.
- White — *Fluid Mechanics* (McGraw-Hill) — Very readable undergraduate text with many worked examples.
- Anderson — *Computational Fluid Dynamics: The Basics with Applications* (McGraw-Hill) — Gentlest introduction to CFD; great for equations and finite differences.
- Versteeg & Malalasekera — *An Introduction to Computational Fluid Dynamics: The Finite Volume Method* (2nd ed.) — The classic FVM book: diffusion, convection schemes, SIMPLE, turbulence.
- Moukalled, Mangani & Darwish — *The Finite Volume Method in Computational Fluid Dynamics* (Springer) — FVM in depth, with OpenFOAM implementation examples.
- Ferziger, Perić & Street — *Computational Methods for Fluid Dynamics* (4th ed., Springer) — Rigorous reference for numerical methods.
- [Greenshields & Weller — *Notes on CFD: General Principles* (free online)](https://doc.cfd.direct/notes/cfd-general-principles/) — Written by OpenFOAM's developers; explains the methods OpenFOAM uses. ★
- Pope — *Turbulent Flows* (Cambridge) — The definitive turbulence textbook.
- Wilcox — *Turbulence Modeling for CFD* (DCW Industries) — Practical reference for RANS models, especially k-ω.
- Davidson — *Turbulence: An Introduction for Scientists and Engineers* (Oxford) — Physical intuition for turbulence with less maths than Pope.
- [VanderPlas — *Python Data Science Handbook* (free online)](https://jakevdp.github.io/PythonDataScienceHandbook/) — NumPy, pandas, Matplotlib, scikit-learn. ★
- [James, Witten, Hastie, Tibshirani & Taylor — *An Introduction to Statistical Learning (with Python)* (free online)](https://www.statlearning.com/) — Best introduction to classic ML concepts. ★
- [Rasmussen & Williams — *Gaussian Processes for Machine Learning* (free online)](https://gaussianprocess.org/gpml/) — The GP reference. ★
- [Bishop & Bishop — *Deep Learning: Foundations and Concepts* (2024, free to read online)](https://www.bishopbook.com/) — Modern, thorough deep-learning textbook. ★
- [Prince — *Understanding Deep Learning* (free online)](https://udlbook.github.io/udlbook/) — Clear, well-illustrated, with notebooks. ★
- [Zhang, Lipton, Li & Smola — *Dive into Deep Learning* (free online)](https://d2l.ai/) — Hands-on with runnable PyTorch code. ★
- [Goodfellow, Bengio & Courville — *Deep Learning* (free online)](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/) — Classic theory reference. ★
- [Brunton & Kutz — *Data-Driven Science and Engineering* (2nd ed., Cambridge)](https://databookuw.com/) — The key book for ML + fluids: SVD/POD, DMD, ROMs, physics-informed ML. Site has videos and code for every section.
- [Thuerey et al. — *Physics-based Deep Learning* (free online)](https://physicsbaseddeeplearning.org/) — Surrogates, PINNs, differentiable physics — with notebooks. ★
- Kutz, Brunton, Brunton & Proctor — *Dynamic Mode Decomposition* (SIAM) — Full treatment of DMD.
- [Hamilton — *Graph Representation Learning* (free pre-print)](https://www.cs.mcgill.ca/~wlh/grl_book/) — Short, clear introduction to GNNs. ★
