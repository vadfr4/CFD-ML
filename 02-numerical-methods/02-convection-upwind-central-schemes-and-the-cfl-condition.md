## Resources

- [Lorena Barba — *CFD Python: 12 steps to Navier–Stokes* (notebooks + video lessons)](https://github.com/barbagroup/CFDPython) — steps 1–4 (convection) — Build a 2D Navier–Stokes solver step by step in Python.
- Versteeg & Malalasekera — *An Introduction to Computational Fluid Dynamics: The Finite Volume Method* (2nd ed.) — ch. 5: convection–diffusion — The classic FVM book: diffusion, convection schemes, SIMPLE, turbulence.
- [Fluid Mechanics 101 (Aidan Wimshurst) — YouTube CFD lectures](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Fluid+Mechanics+101+Aidan+Wimshurst) — Excellent short lectures on FVM, SIMPLE, turbulence models, y+ and wall functions.
