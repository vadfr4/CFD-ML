## Resources

**Books**

- [Prince — *Understanding Deep Learning* (free online)](https://udlbook.github.io/udlbook/) — Clear, well-illustrated, with notebooks.
- [Zhang, Lipton, Li & Smola — *Dive into Deep Learning* (free online)](https://d2l.ai/) — Hands-on with runnable PyTorch code.
- [Bishop & Bishop — *Deep Learning: Foundations and Concepts* (2024, free to read online)](https://www.bishopbook.com/) — Modern, thorough deep-learning textbook.
- [Goodfellow, Bengio & Courville — *Deep Learning* (free online)](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/) — Classic theory reference.

**Videos & courses**

- [Andrej Karpathy — *Neural Networks: Zero to Hero*](https://karpathy.ai/zero-to-hero.html) — Build backprop and neural nets from scratch.
- [3Blue1Brown — *Neural networks* series](https://www.3blue1brown.com/topics/neural-networks) — Visual intuition for gradient descent and backprop.
- [Stanford CS231n — *Deep Learning for Computer Vision* (CNNs)](https://cs231n.stanford.edu/) — Course notes; lectures are on YouTube.

**Docs & tools**

- [PyTorch tutorials](https://pytorch.org/tutorials/)
