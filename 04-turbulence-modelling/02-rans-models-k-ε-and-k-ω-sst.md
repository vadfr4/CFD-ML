## Resources

- Wilcox — *Turbulence Modeling for CFD* (DCW Industries) — Practical reference for RANS models, especially k-ω.
- Versteeg & Malalasekera — *An Introduction to Computational Fluid Dynamics: The Finite Volume Method* (2nd ed.) — ch. 3: turbulence — The classic FVM book: diffusion, convection schemes, SIMPLE, turbulence.
- [NASA Turbulence Modeling Resource](https://turbmodels.larc.nasa.gov/) — k-ε and SST pages — Exact model definitions and validation cases.
- [Fluid Mechanics 101 (Aidan Wimshurst) — YouTube CFD lectures](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Fluid+Mechanics+101+Aidan+Wimshurst) — turbulence model lectures — Excellent short lectures on FVM, SIMPLE, turbulence models, y+ and wall functions.
- More: [subject resources](README.md#resources)
