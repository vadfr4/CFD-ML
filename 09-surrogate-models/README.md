## Resources

**Books**

- [Thuerey et al. — *Physics-based Deep Learning* (free online)](https://physicsbaseddeeplearning.org/) — Surrogates, PINNs, differentiable physics — with notebooks.
- [Brunton & Kutz — *Data-Driven Science and Engineering* (2nd ed., Cambridge)](https://databookuw.com/) — The key book for ML + fluids: SVD/POD, DMD, ROMs, physics-informed ML. Site has videos and code for every section.

**Videos & courses**

- [Nils Thuerey — physics-based deep learning talks](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Nils+Thuerey+physics+based+deep+learning)

**Papers**

- [Thuerey et al. (2020) — *Deep Learning Methods for RANS Simulations of Airfoil Flows* (AIAA J.)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.08217) — Code: https://github.com/thunil/Deep-Flow-Prediction
- [Ronneberger, Fischer & Brox (2015) — *U-Net*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.04597)
- Hesthaven & Ubbiali (2018) — *Non-intrusive reduced order modeling of nonlinear problems using neural networks* (J. Comput. Phys.) — POD-NN
- [Brunton, Noack & Koumoutsakos (2020) — *Machine Learning for Fluid Mechanics* (Annu. Rev. Fluid Mech.)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.11075)
