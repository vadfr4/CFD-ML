## Resources

**Books**

- [Thuerey et al. — *Physics-based Deep Learning* (free online)](https://physicsbaseddeeplearning.org/) — Surrogates, PINNs, differentiable physics — with notebooks.
- [Brunton & Kutz — *Data-Driven Science and Engineering* (2nd ed., Cambridge)](https://databookuw.com/) — The key book for ML + fluids: SVD/POD, DMD, ROMs, physics-informed ML. Site has videos and code for every section.

**Videos & courses**

- [Steve Brunton — *Physics Informed Machine Learning* lecture series](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Steve+Brunton+Physics+Informed+Machine+Learning) — PINNs, DeepONet, FNO and GNN lectures.

**Papers**

- [Raissi, Perdikaris & Karniadakis (2019) — *Physics-informed neural networks* (J. Comput. Phys.); pre-print part I](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.10561)
- [Karniadakis et al. (2021) — *Physics-informed machine learning* (Nat. Rev. Phys.)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42254-021-00314-5)
- [Wang, Teng & Perdikaris (2021) — *Understanding and mitigating gradient pathologies in PINNs*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.04536)

**Docs & tools**

- [DeepXDE documentation](https://deepxde.readthedocs.io/)
