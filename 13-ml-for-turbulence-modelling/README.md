## Resources

**Books**

- Pope — *Turbulent Flows* (Cambridge) — The definitive turbulence textbook.

**Papers**

- [Duraisamy, Iaccarino & Xiao (2019) — *Turbulence Modeling in the Age of Data* (Annu. Rev. Fluid Mech.)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.00183)
- [Ling, Kurzawski & Templeton (2016) — *RANS turbulence modelling using deep neural networks with embedded invariance* (JFM) — TBNN](https://www.osti.gov/pages/servlets/purl/1333570)
- [McConkey, Yee & Lien (2021) — *A curated dataset for data-driven turbulence modelling* (Sci. Data)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-021-01034-2)
- [Kochkov et al. (2021) — *Machine learning–accelerated computational fluid dynamics* (PNAS)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.01010)

**Docs & tools**

- [NASA Turbulence Modeling Resource](https://turbmodels.larc.nasa.gov/) — Exact model definitions and validation cases.
